How to protect your rental deposit

Most deposit disputes come down to one question: “Was this scratch there when you moved in?” The cheapest and strongest answer is a set of dated photos taken on day one.

On move-in day

  1. Before unpacking, photograph every room corner to corner: walls, floor, ceiling, windows.
  2. Take close-ups of defects: scratches, stains, cracked tiles, damp. Put a pen next to them for scale.
  3. Shoot the kitchen and bathroom separately: inside cupboards, taps, hob, fridge.
  4. Photograph the meters (electricity, water, gas).
  5. Walk through the whole place in one short video.

Turn the photos into proof

The date on a phone photo is easy to change, and a landlord can dispute it. The same day, add the photos to the Handover pack on the File Proof page. Your photos stay on your phone; only their fingerprints are anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain. You get two small files: a pack list (.json) and a proof (.ots).

Share and keep

  • Email the photos and both proof files to your landlord. That also records that they were informed that day.
  • Keep everything in one folder with a cloud backup.

On move-out day

Shoot the same angles again and make a second pack. If there is a dispute, you put the two packs side by side; anyone can check in the Check tab which photo existed on which day and that none was changed.

This is not a notarisation or a qualified electronic timestamp; a court weighs it as evidence. For high-value disputes, also consider an official inspection report.
