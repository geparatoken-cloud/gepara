1.3 The Service

The Service proves that a digital file existed at a point in time and has not changed since. The fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your file is computed in your browser and registered with independent timestamping services (OpenTimestamps). Your file itself is never uploaded. You can then download a PDF proof report with a QR verification link. Checking a proof is always free.

1.6 Nature and limits of the proof

A proof is not a notarisation or a qualified electronic timestamp. Courts and other parties weigh it as evidence at their discretion. It does not prove what a photo shows or where it was taken.
